Our Approach

Simple Price Action. Nothing Complicated.

You don't need complex data or expensive tools to trade well. Our analysis is based on what the chart shows: price, candles, and key levels.

  • A few candlestick patterns that work for intraday
  • The support & resistance levels that actually work for our setup
  • A simple way to identify the trend

What we don't use

  • Option chain data
  • Open interest (OI) analysis
  • Option Greeks & complex calculations
  • Multiple complicated indicators
  • Paid tools or premium subscriptions
