1. Introduction

This policy explains how Index Option (Yogi) collects and uses your personal information when you visit this website, enroll in the course, or contact us.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you enroll or contact us:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number.
  • Your Telegram username.
  • Messages you send us.

Payments are processed by Cosmofeed, a third-party platform. We do not collect or store your card, UPI, or bank details.

3. How We Use It

We use your information to:

  • Give you access to the course and the private group.
  • Respond to your questions.
  • Send course-related updates.
  • Improve the course.

4. We Don't Sell Your Data

Your personal data is never sold or rented to anyone.

5. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services, including Cosmofeed (enrollment & payments), Telegram, WhatsApp, YouTube (for the intro video), and GitHub Pages (hosting). These services have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to read them.

6. Cookies

This website does not use tracking cookies. The embedded YouTube video may set cookies when played; we use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode to reduce this.

If we add analytics in the future, we will update this policy.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no transmission over the internet is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

We keep your information only as long as needed for course access, support, or legal reasons.

9. Your Rights

You can ask to access, correct, or delete your personal data by emailing us. We handle such requests in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

10. Children

This website and course are not intended for anyone under 18.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page.
